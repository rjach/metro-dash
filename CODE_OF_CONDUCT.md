# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Metro Dash to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its players.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information (including camera footage) without explicit permission.
- Trolling or sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through a [GitHub security advisory](../../security/advisories/new), or by contacting the repository owner. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
