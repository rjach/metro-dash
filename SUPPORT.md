# Getting help

- **Questions, ideas, show-and-tell:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/rjach/metro-dash/discussions).
- **Bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/rjach/metro-dash/issues/new/choose) with the bug template. For camera problems, include your browser, OS and what the camera setup screen said. Please don't attach video of yourself.
- **Security or privacy issues:** report privately; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
